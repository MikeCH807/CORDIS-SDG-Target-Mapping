BRIDGE CLASSIFICATION
Classify target_action_bridge as exactly one of:
- DIRECT: the project itself performs, implements, delivers, or applies the governing target action/intervention for the relevant object or beneficiary.
- ENABLING: the project does not itself deliver the final target action/outcome, but evidence establishes a concrete, target-specific causal mechanism, capability, intervention-development step, tool, method, infrastructure, training activity, or bottleneck removal that substantively advances that action/outcome.
- ABSENT: only thematic similarity, generic benefit, broad relevance, or speculative repurposing connects the project to the Target.

ENABLING IS NOT GENERIC
Research-stage or indirect work can be ELIGIBLE when evidence explicitly shows what target-relevant property, mechanism, capability, intervention, or bottleneck it improves. Do not demand verbatim repetition of Target wording. Infer a bridge only from an evidenced mechanism, not from generic innovation, sustainability, resilience, digitalisation, research, monitoring, training, efficiency, or technology-development language alone.

Examples in principle:
- explicitly reducing energy use in a process can enable an energy-efficiency component even if one project cannot move a global indicator;
- explicitly developing restoration/conservation decision support can enable restoration/conservation action even if deployment is later;
- generic 'green', 'smart', 'efficient', 'innovative', or 'resilient' technology without the target-specific mechanism remains ABSENT.

DISTINGUISH RESTRICTIONS FROM EMPHASIS
Do not convert non-exclusive emphasis into an exclusion criterion. By contrast, wording that defines the beneficiary or instrument itself (for example 'for least developed countries', 'for women and girls', a specified education stage, or a specified intervention class) remains integral to the component it governs.
